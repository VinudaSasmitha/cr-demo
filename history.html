About CR & FC

Welcome to the Ceylonese Rugby & Football Club (CR & FC), the premier destination for sports, entertainment, and camaraderie in Sri Lanka. Widely acknowledged both locally and abroad, our club seamlessly blends a century of rich history with state-of-the-art modern facilities.

Our Historic Legacy

As we turn back the pages of history, the roots of CR & FC take us back to 1922. The club was born out of a passionate clamor for the recognition of Ceylonese ruggerites. Our founding father, Colonel Ernest Huntley Joseph, initially gathered and coached a team to play on the Non-descript Cricket Club (N.C.C.) grounds. The instant success of these fixtures prompted Colonel Joseph to canvass public support and officially form a truly Ceylonese Rugby & Football Club.

Displaying our proud regimental colors of Red and Blue, the club quickly grew in stature. Adhering to steadfast principles, dignity, and the highest traditions of the game, our legendary teams—popularly known as the ‘Red Shirts’—have produced generations of high-profile, outstanding players who have gone on to represent the country with immense distinction.

A Tradition of Champions

Excellence on the field is woven into the very fabric of CR & FC. We are incredibly proud to celebrate our latest milestone: becoming the triple Champions of the 2025/26 Rugby Season, securing both the SLR Maliban Interclub Rugby Tournament and the prestigious Clifford Cup.

"Securing the triple championship is a testament to the dedication of our players and the enduring spirit of the Red Shirts."

To ensure this legacy continues, we are currently in the process of establishing a comprehensive Rugby Academy. This initiative is dedicated to developing rugby standards, nurturing aspiring ruggerites, and grooming the next generation of players to attain national and international recognition.

The Entertainment Hub of Colombo

In 2023, a major milestone was achieved with the installation of cutting-edge floodlights at our grounds at Longdon Place. Since then, all CR & FC home rugby matches are played under the lights, creating a captivating, carnival-like evening atmosphere.

Beyond sports, CR & FC has evolved into the most secure and vibrant venue hub in Colombo City. We proudly host:
